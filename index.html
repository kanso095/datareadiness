AI Data Readiness for SMEs

A business goal-oriented approach to preparing small and medium enterprises for AI adoption through strategic data readiness assessment.

Read Abstract View Benefits

Research Abstract

Following the AI boom in 2025, AI-powered tools and services have rapidly emerged within large-scale organizations. They empower enterprises to reshape their businesses, resources, and strategies based on AI's capabilities. Although AI-powered tools and agents are sophisticated enough to serve general business cases, they lack the specific talent to provide in-depth assistance without access to sufficient business data.

This research will dive into academic databases, peer-reviewed journals, and reputable industry sources to identify key business goals for SMEs and then determine the specific data readiness dimensions related to each business goal.

The Problem

AI cannot function without data. Data readiness is becoming a key research area for enabling enterprises to understand how well an organization is connected to the AI world.

Existing literature primarily focuses on data readiness at a general management level, analyzing the impact of people, processes, and IT infrastructure. However, the connection between this form of readiness and specific business goals is relatively weak.

In today's rapidly evolving environment, especially for SMEs, achieving business goals is crucial for survival and development. Therefore, it is beneficial for SMEs to understand their data readiness from a business goal-oriented perspective.

Directly investing in the customization of AI tools does not seem practical for SMEs, considering the cost of talent, hardware, and software subscriptions. This approach does not fit the current state of most SMEs, which have limited resources.

The AI Adoption Gap

High adoption rate but low transformation impact

Methodology

This research aims to strengthen the connection between business strategy and data strategy. As one of the intersections between business strategy and data strategy, business goals can provide common ground.

It establishes a protocol among business owners, operations managers, IT specialists, customer experience managers, and product managers to plan and modify AI-specific data strategies that align with business strategies.

Benefit ID Description
B1 Direct reduction in wasted expenditure on inappropriate AI tools and misguided data projects.
B2 Drastically shortens the time to develop and approve a first-pass, AI-specific data strategy.
B3 Ensures every data initiative is explicitly justified by a top business goal.
B4 Directs limited personnel effort only towards improving critical data dimensions.
B5 Builds cross-functional buy-in and creates shared understanding of data.
B6 Transforms the AI journey from high-risk gamble to managed process.

Results

Shift Investment from Tools to Data Foundation

Current State

Resources are wasted evaluating and customizing expensive AI tools without the data to power them effectively.

Future State

SME investment is prioritised on preparing high-quality, business-specific data first, creating a valuable asset for any AI tool.

Create a Direct Strategy-to-Execution Link

Current State

Business strategy and data strategy are disconnected, leading to misaligned projects and lack of buy-in.

Future State

Every data requirement is directly justified by a specific business goal, ensuring strategic alignment and clear value.

Simplify AI Data Strategy Development

Current State

Data readiness is seen as a complex, technical discipline requiring expensive experts.

Future State

SME owners and operational managers are empowered with a simplified, goal-oriented template.

Break Down Functional Silos

Current State

Data is managed in a technical silo (IT), while business goals live in another (Leadership).

Future State

A cross-functional protocol establishes a common language, ensuring collaboration between business owners, operations, IT, and customer-facing roles.

De-risk and Accelerate AI Adoption

Current State

The AI journey is perceived as high-risk and complex, leading to paralysis or expensive false starts.

Future State

SMEs have a clear, proven, and incremental pathway to prepare for AI, significantly reducing project failure risk.
