Abstract

Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs) face significant challenges in achieving data readiness due to resource constraints and the generic nature of existing frameworks. This systematic literature review examines data readiness dimensions specifically tailored to SME business goals, analyzing 82 core academic papers from a comprehensive search of 3,247 publications spanning 2004-2025, supplemented by 10 major industry reports.


Through rigorous analysis, we identify the top 10 business goals for SMEs and map them to 8 consolidated data readiness dimensions, each supported by empirical evidence and specific citations. The research reveals that Data Analytics Capability correlates with all 10 goals (100% coverage), while Data Quality supports 9 out of 10 goals (90% coverage).


The findings demonstrate that SMEs can achieve significant performance improvements—including 25-40% gains in customer retention, 15-30% increases in sales, and 20-35% operational efficiency improvements—by systematically building data readiness aligned with their core business goals.
